Service Bus queues explained

Azure Service Bus is a fully managed enterprise message broker with message queues and publish-subscribe topics. Queues decouple producers from consumers, so a slow consumer never blocks the sender, and messages survive restarts of either side. Paragraph 1 adds more detail about dead-letter queues, sessions and duplicate detection.
